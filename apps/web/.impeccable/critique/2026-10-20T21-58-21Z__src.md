## Overall Impression

The app feels calm and practical, with a consistent identity and strong operational foundations. The biggest opportunity is to make the parent and volunteer entry points feel more self-explanatory, especially on a phone and when the volunteer starts from the generic check-in link.

## What’s Working

- **The visual system fits the product.** The wordmark, blue shell, plum actions, and restrained surfaces keep the club identity present without competing with the work.
- **Registration anticipates parent concerns.** Eligibility is visible before personal details, labels are explicit, privacy information is available, and the draft behavior is disclosed.
- **Trial-day tools support recovery.** Scan and Lookup are distinct, lookup results include the player’s number, and volunteers can undo the last check-in.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Generic volunteer entry requires an Event ID

At `/check-in`, volunteers are asked to enter an Event ID and PIN. The public “Volunteer check-in” link opens this generic route; the event-specific link exists in the admin workflow but is not apparent from that entry point.

**Why it matters:** A volunteer without the ID cannot start, creating an avoidable dependency on the coordinator and a chance of choosing the wrong event.

**Fix:** Make an event-specific volunteer link easier to distribute, or let volunteers choose a readable event before entering the PIN. Keep the PIN as the separate access credential.  
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape src/checkin/CheckInPage.tsx`

### [P2] The first registration field is near the bottom of the mobile opening view

In the 390 × 844 px capture, the first text input begins around y=803 px, after the event details, eligibility summary, draft notice, privacy disclosure, policy link, and section navigation.

**Why it matters:** A parent sees nearly a screen of setup before they can begin entering details. The eligibility information is important, but the page can feel longer than expected.

**Fix:** Keep the eligibility check up front, but reduce the surrounding preamble; move lower-priority privacy or policy details below the first fields or into disclosure.  
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable onboard src/routes/RegisterPage.tsx`

### [P2] The all-round schedule view can be hard to scan on a phone

The Full Game List defaults to **All rounds**, and each round renders repeated court/team tables. The fixture had only two rounds and two courts; the product brief describes larger events.

**Why it matters:** At realistic event sizes, reaching a specific round or court may require substantial scrolling.

**Fix:** Preserve **All rounds** as the default, as requested. Improve the all-round experience with a compact overview or round-jump navigation before the detailed tables.  
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout src/planner/FullGameListPage.tsx`

### [P2] The public home does not explain what happens after registration

The home page presents trial details and a Register action, but does not preview the existing next steps: the secure Checkout link by email, then the ticket/QR code used at check-in.

**Why it matters:** First-time parents may hesitate before entering a child’s and guardian’s details if they do not know what happens next.

**Fix:** Add a short, factual “What happens next” explanation near the event list or registration action.  
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify src/routes/PublicHomePage.tsx`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (power user):** On Full Game List, correcting a slot requires stepping through “Player to move,” “Destination slot,” then “Move or substitute.” There is no visible bulk or shortcut path for repeated changes.
- **Sam (accessibility-dependent):** Reviewed controls have text labels and captures show a focus ring. However, keyboard-only and screen-reader behavior were not tested; the long mobile schedule and scrollable tables merit a focused accessibility check.
- **Casey (distracted mobile user):** The first registration input starts around y=803 px in the 390 px fixture. Closing the tab clears the draft, so an interruption that leads to closing it can cost the parent their entered details.

## Minor Observations

- Admin Events formats dates as `2027-09-12`; public listings use a human-readable date.
- Full Game List repeats revision information in two places.
- Admin Events’ “Could not load events” error has no retry action.
- The mobile Event tools menu has nine links, grouped 4/3/2. That grouping helps, and all three groups should remain expanded per the chosen design; improve scanability without hiding them.
- The browser fixtures used one event, 28 players, two courts, and two rounds. Larger schedule concerns are risks inferred from the product brief, not measurements at full roster size.
- Payment/checkout, ticket, login/verification, print sheets, and the full planner/review workflows were not assessed in depth. The scanner’s camera was replaced by a test decoder. This was not a real-device, screen-reader, slow-network, or user test.

## Questions to Consider

- What information must a parent see before the first field, and what can move below it?
- How can the all-round schedule stay the default while making a specific round faster to reach?
