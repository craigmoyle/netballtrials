## Priority Issues

### 1. [P1] PIN validation feedback is outside the action viewport

**Evidence:** Entering `1` and clicking Set or rotate PIN produces the correct local error, but it renders after all court-color editors. With four courts, the alert was roughly 2,100px down on desktop and 2,460px down on mobile. Focus and scroll remained near the button.

**Why it matters:** A sighted coordinator sees no apparent response and may repeat the action or assume it failed silently. `role="alert"` alone does not make feedback visible.

**Fix:** Show the error beneath the PIN input, associate it with the field, and display “4–8 digits” before submission. Move shared operations feedback above court colors.

**Location:** `apps/web/src/admin/EventControls.tsx:59–62, 139–141`  
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### 2. [P2] The payment button promises a different next step

**Evidence:** Both registration forms say Continue to payment. Mocked successful responses instead show Request received or Checkout link sent, directing the parent to email rather than opening checkout.

**Why it matters:** Parents can mistake the email handoff for a payment-page failure; Admins may expect to take payment on their own device.

**Fix:** Use public Request payment link and Admin Send payment link, with nearby wording explaining the parent/guardian email handoff. Preserve the public response’s privacy protections.

**Location:** `apps/web/src/routes/RegisterPage.tsx:146–154, 292–294`  
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### 3. [P2] Revising an earlier preference can leave duplicate ranks selected

**Evidence:** Choose first position GS, second GA, then change first to GA. Both controls display Goal attack. The second selected option becomes disabled but remains selected, with no local warning. Reproduced on desktop and mobile without submitting the conflict.

**Why it matters:** The disabled-option safeguard appears to prevent duplicates but does not handle revisions to earlier choices.

**Fix:** Revalidate distinct, non-empty ranks on change and before submission. Mark the conflicting rank and ask for correction rather than silently deleting the preference.

**Location:** `apps/web/src/routes/RegisterPage.tsx:96–104, 257–266`  
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`
