## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The Chisholm wordmark, blue/plum palette, trial-day vocabulary and selector workflows give the app a clear identity. The new Event operations route also makes the Admin area feel more task-oriented. The remaining weakness is not branding: frequent actions and long forms still demand more scrolling and attention than ideal.

**Deterministic scan:** 23 advisory findings, all in `apps/web/src/styles.css`: 17 `design-system-color` and 6 `design-system-font-size`, at lines 31, 37, 139, 161, 210–214, 255, 273, 278, 289, 297, 300 and 348. ## Overall Impression

The major navigation and data-loss problems identified in the prior critique are substantially improved. The remaining opportunity is to keep high-frequency tasks prominent and help parents understand the length of registration before they begin.

## What’s Working

- Event settings and Event operations are separate; PIN and court-color controls are now reachable without scrolling through the configuration form.
- The shared workflow navigation has clear Event, Planning and Trial day groups, with a compact, current-page-labeled mobile disclosure.
- An event-specific volunteer link shows a matching event name/date. Editing the ID clears that preview, while keeping the PIN required and masked.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P2] Mobile registration has no progress cue.**  
   **Why it matters:** The inspected form is about 2,320px tall, and “Continue to payment” is at the end. A first-time parent who is interrupted has no quick sense of how much remains.  
   **Fix:** Add lightweight progress or section anchors for the four existing groups, keeping the single-page flow.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

2. **[P2] Admin check-in is still low on the Event operations page.**  
   **Why it matters:** With two courts, the page is about 1,593px tall; the Admin check-in link follows lifecycle, PIN and court-color controls. The committee’s frequent trial-day action still requires a substantial scroll.  
   **Fix:** Move conditional Admin check-in and day-of registration links into a quick-actions area near the event name/date.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

3. **[P3] Special trial-day routes lack a desktop active marker.**  
   **Why it matters:** The mobile Event tools button names Admin check-in or day-of registration, but the desktop workflow groups do not mark those special routes because they are conditional actions rather than ordinary links.  
   **Fix:** Show a non-link current-page marker in the Trial day group on those routes.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan, first-time parent:** The form is divided into four clear groups, but there is no progress indicator on a long mobile page.
- **Alex, committee operator:** PIN and court colors are easier to reach, but Admin check-in still sits after those sections.
- **Casey, mobile volunteer:** The shared link identifies the trial when it can be matched. If verification fails, the message is advisory and the volunteer must confirm the ID with the coordinator before trying a PIN.

## Minor Observations

- Event settings remains about 2,688px tall on desktop, though the remaining bottom section is the infrequent Delete event action.
- Event operations reports a load error and suggests refreshing rather than offering an in-page retry.
- The expanded mobile menu is grouped and had no horizontal overflow in the inspected viewport.

## Questions to Consider

- Would a four-section progress cue help parents finish registration without turning the form into a wizard?
- Should Admin check-in be the first quick action on Event operations, ahead of less frequent status and court-color changes?
