## Overall Impression

Calm and credible for a committee tool. The preview-before-send workflow and clear eligibility information build trust. The biggest opportunity is smoothing the long route into registration and improving mobile data comparison.

## What’s Working

- The club palette and netball-specific language give the interface a clear identity without decorative noise.
- Communications makes recipient selection safer with eligibility states, a persistent count and a review step before sending.
- Player History warns Admins that an exact match is not proof of identity and keeps unrelated family and payment details out of results.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P2] Mobile comparison tables hide important columns and compress event details.** At 390px, Communications shows only part of the email column, while association, status and positions are off-screen. In Player History, the event cell fragments into many short lines and other comparison metrics are hidden. **Fix:** retain table semantics, give the tables a readable minimum width, and add a clear horizontal-scroll cue (or an intentional mobile comparison layout). **Location:** `apps/web/src/admin/MessagesPage.tsx:279–291`, `apps/web/src/admin/PlayerHistoryPage.tsx:42–61`, `apps/web/src/styles.css:282, 368–380, 462`. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`.

2. **[P2] Registration puts a long disclosure runway before the first field.** On a phone, event details, eligibility and two privacy paragraphs precede section navigation and player details. This is important information, but it delays the first step of a parent’s task. **Fix:** keep essential eligibility and privacy points prominent, but consider a concise summary with full details available on demand, and bring section navigation/fields higher. **Location:** `apps/web/src/routes/RegisterPage.tsx:413–440`. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.

3. **[P2] The desktop roster is narrower than its data needs.** The seven-column table sits inside the shared 62rem section cap, leaving unused space on a 1280px screen while parent details and statuses wrap. That makes a long operational roster slower to scan. **Fix:** widen this data-heavy view or tune its columns without widening unrelated pages. **Location:** `apps/web/src/styles.css:120`, `apps/web/src/admin/RosterPage.tsx:92–110`. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex, power user:** Bulk selection, filters and templates are useful, but Communications remains a long page; delivery history and scheduled items sit well below the send workflow. There are no keyboard shortcuts.
- **Sam, accessibility-dependent user:** Keyboard focus and labeled controls are strong. On mobile, though, the recipient and history tables provide little visual indication that additional columns can be reached by scrolling.
