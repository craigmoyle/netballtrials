## Overall Impression

The core workflow is clear, and the new Player History screen appropriately warns that exact name/date-of-birth matches are not proof of identity. The biggest design opportunity is reducing the amount users must scroll through on Communications and registration without hiding important privacy or recipient information.

## What’s Working

- **Communications is explicit about audience and recipient selection.** Filters, individual checkboxes, player Numbers and a final recipient preview give Admins useful safeguards.
- **History does not silently merge players.** Its warning and exact-match search are a sensible privacy-conscious first step.
- **Game views are directly visible.** Full Game List, Position View, Player Statistics and Player Reference are selectable without the old dropdown; opponent variety is reported with the player statistics.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P2] Communications makes compose/send follow a long recipient list.** Recipient rows come before templates, composing, scheduling and history. At a real 70-player event, this becomes a long page even when filters are available.
   - **Why it matters:** Admins have to scroll between choosing recipients and acting on a message, especially on a phone.
   - **Fix:** Keep filtering and selection, but place recipients in a bounded scroll area with a persistent selected count and make compose/send controls easier to return to. Keep the final preview as the confirmation point.
   - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

2. **[P2] The registration privacy notice is long and produces short orphaned lines on mobile.** The browser detector flagged five stragglers, including endings such as “details.” and “schedule.” The notice also pushes the form lower on a phone.
   - **Why it matters:** Parents need privacy information before entering a child’s details, but a long wall of copy makes both notice and form harder to scan.
   - **Fix:** Tighten the visible notice while retaining the operator, purposes, providers, overseas-processing caveat, retention period and access/correction contact. Keep fuller privacy details available before submission.
   - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

3. **[P2] Player History’s mobile table has no visible horizontal-scroll instruction.** At 390px it stays inside the page without causing page overflow, but most of its nine columns are offscreen.
   - **Why it matters:** Court history and opponent-variety data can be missed if the Admin doesn’t know the table scrolls sideways.
   - **Fix:** Add a short “Scroll horizontally to view all history details” cue, or display each event as a stacked record on narrow screens while preserving the full desktop table.
   - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Casey, distracted mobile user:** Communications requires navigating a lengthy list before reaching the message form; there is no short route back to compose.
- **Sam, accessibility-dependent user:** The Player History table is keyboard-focusable, but no visible prompt says more columns are available horizontally.
- **Jordan, first-time parent:** The privacy notice is clear about providers and retention, but its length and orphaned lines make the practical takeaways harder to scan.

## Minor Observations

- In Associations, contact name and email are described as optional, but the form requires both together or neither. Explain this beside the fields.
- Player History is a manual exact-match lookup, not an automatic multi-year player profile. That matches the current privacy-conscious scope but limits progression history when a name or date of birth is entered differently.

2. For recipient selection, would you prefer a bounded independently scrolling list with a persistent selected count, or paginated results?
3. Should player history remain an exact-match search with manual interpretation, or do you foresee wanting Admin-confirmed persistent links across seasons?
