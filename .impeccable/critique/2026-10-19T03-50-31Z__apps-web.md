## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The workflows are distinctly Chisholm Netball: eligibility rules, positions, bib and court colors, volunteer PINs, check-in, and game selectors all fit this trial process. The blue/plum palette and restrained “sideline clipboard” style are coherent. The page structures are conventional forms, navigation, and tables; much of the product’s character comes from its content and rules rather than a distinctive visual language.

**Deterministic scan:** `impeccable detect --json apps/web` returned **0 findings** (`[]`), with no rules or file/line locations to report. This is consistent with a clean token/markup scan, but does not catch the qualitative density and hierarchy issues below.

**Visual overlays:** The isolated browser injection preflight succeeded, and the detector script reported `[impeccable] No anti-patterns found.` No user-visible **[Human]** tab was available, so there is no overlay for you to inspect. The scanner’s repeated ZXing `NotFoundException` messages came from the blank fake camera stream used for testing, not from a page error.

## Overall Impression

The app feels calm, purposeful, and operationally credible. Parent registration and staff workflows both have sensible safeguards. The biggest opportunity is reducing the amount of scrolling and decision-making required on mobile without changing the approved single-page, memory-only registration behavior or the all-round schedule default.

## What’s Working

- **Useful parent-facing context:** event details, fee, age range, policy, and privacy information support informed registration.
- **Good operational recovery:** check-in offers lookup, retry, named outcomes, and undo; schedule changes have clear consequences and leave warnings.
- **Consistent identity:** the logo and blue/plum palette establish Chisholm without decoration competing with dense operational data.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Registration takes a long time to reach its first field on mobile.** The tested form is about 2,738px tall at 390px wide. Introductory event, privacy, and policy material precedes the form; the full eligibility criteria and submission come later. It warns that leaving loses entered details, but cannot restore them.  
   **Why it matters:** A parent using a phone has a long, interruption-prone task and may have to start over.  
   **Fix:** Keep registration as one page and retain memory-only handling. Tighten the material before the first field, make section progress and jump links more prominent, and show a concise eligibility summary near the start while keeping the full criteria on the registration page.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

2. **[P2] The all-round Full Game List is a long mobile scroll.** Even the mocked two-round, two-court plan rendered to about 4,708px at 390px. The round filter is at the top, so returning to it takes extra scrolling.  
   **Why it matters:** Volunteers commonly need a specific round, court, or player while working under time pressure.  
   **Fix:** Preserve **All rounds** as the default, but make the round filter easier to reach and consider round jump links or collapsible round sections.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

3. **[P2] Desktop event navigation presents nine destinations together.** The Event, Planning, and Trial day groups help, and mobile collapses the navigation, but desktop still gives nine links similar prominence.  
   **Why it matters:** Staff must repeatedly choose among many destinations and can lose the sense of their current task.  
   **Fix:** Keep the routes visible and grouped as approved; strengthen the active-workflow cue and lower the emphasis of less-used links.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

4. **[P2] The scanner gives little guidance before the first scan.** A camera preview and manual-lookup link are present, but there is no clear “camera ready” cue or instruction for positioning a ticket.  
   **Why it matters:** A volunteer may not know whether the camera started or what to do next during check-in.  
   **Fix:** Add a short scan instruction and visible camera permission/ready states, keeping manual lookup easy to find.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable onboard`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan, first-time guardian:** The event and policy links help, but the first field comes after a substantial information block; full eligibility details appear near the end. Keep the criteria on registration, but make the summary easier to find before entering details.
- **Casey, interrupted mobile user:** The long registration page cannot be resumed after a lost tab. The app appropriately keeps personal details in memory only, so the improvement should shorten and clarify the task rather than add browser storage.
- **Alex, volunteer/power user:** The round selector and alternate views help, but navigating the all-round mobile schedule still involves substantial scrolling. Round jump links could reduce that effort without changing the all-round default.

## Minor Observations

- Lookup’s initial empty state could say when searching begins and what to enter.
- “No trials currently scheduled” could offer a relevant next step.
- The visual review covered public home, registration, event operations, Full Game List, lookup, and scanner at 1280px and 390px using mocked data. No horizontal overflow, page errors, unhandled mock routes, or real API writes were observed.
