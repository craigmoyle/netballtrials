## Overall Impression

The latest changes improve registration entry, keep round controls reachable, clarify the active event workflow, and give volunteers a clear scanner-ready cue. The biggest remaining opportunity is making the two longest phone tasks—registration and schedule review—feel quicker to enter and navigate.

## What’s Working

- **Registration now surfaces eligibility early** and keeps the full criteria beside the confirmation. The policy link remains conditional on having a URL.
- **Full Game List keeps All rounds as the default** while the round filter stays available during scrolling.
- **Check-in recovery is reassuring:** the camera has ready instructions, manual lookup remains available, and undo still requires the volunteer to select **Scan same code again** before the same QR is accepted.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Registration’s first field is still low on very small phones.** At 320×720, the first player field is below the initial viewport; at 390×844 it is only just reached. The page’s event details, eligibility summary, privacy cue, policy link, and section navigation all precede data entry.  
   **Why it matters:** Parents using a narrow phone must scroll before starting, and may not know how much of the form is ahead.  
   **Fix:** Keep the approved single-page form, full eligibility criteria, and memory-only handling. Tighten the introductory content further for narrow screens without removing the policy or privacy choices.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

2. **[P2] A registration interrupted by the browser still cannot be resumed.** The leave warning is clear, but it cannot recover details if the tab is discarded or the phone closes the page.  
   **Why it matters:** This is a long form containing child and guardian details. Starting over can be frustrating.  
   **Fix:** Within the approved memory-only constraint, focus on shortening the path and making the no-save warning clear before entry. Do not add browser storage for personal details.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

3. **[P2] Full Game List still puts several controls ahead of assignments on mobile.** Event context, revision history, game view, round filter, and roster changes come before the first round. The sticky filter helps after scrolling, but not with reaching the schedule initially.  
   **Why it matters:** Volunteers looking for a court assignment must pass through management controls first.  
   **Fix:** Reduce the mobile preamble or make secondary revision/roster controls collapsible. Keep **All rounds** as the default.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

4. **[P2] Manual lookup results are loose and can wrap inconsistently.** Names and check-in actions do not form stable rows at 390px.  
   **Why it matters:** Volunteers need to associate the correct person with the action quickly, especially when names are similar.  
   **Fix:** Use a consistent result row with the player name and available number grouped together, and the check-in action aligned separately. Keep a checked-in state distinct.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

5. **[P3] The desktop round label sits beside its select with little separation.** “Team view round” runs directly into the control, unlike the stacked “Game view” label.  
   **Why it matters:** The association is understandable, but looks cramped and inconsistent.  
   **Fix:** Stack the label and select at desktop widths or give the filter a deliberate aligned layout.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`
