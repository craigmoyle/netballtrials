## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The navigation labels and groups—Event, Planning, Trial day; Player, Parent / guardian, Positions, Eligibility; and Staff & volunteers—fit the trials workflow. The club logo and blue/plum shell give it a Chisholm identity. The visual treatment is otherwise conventional and doesn’t yet give the navigation a distinctive feel.

## Overall Impression

The navigation is organized and functional, but consistency is its main weakness: some menus identify the current location, others don’t. The mobile Event tools menu also expands into a long list that takes over much of the screen. There’s room to make the hierarchy feel more deliberate while preserving the visible staff and volunteer routes.

## What’s Working

- **Event navigation is grouped by task:** Event, Planning, and Trial day make the nine destinations easier to scan.
- **The mobile Event tools toggle is informative:** it names the current route and exposes `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- **Registration section links are compact and useful:** four anchors map to the form sections and identify the current section.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P2] The current location isn’t marked consistently.** Event workflow and registration menus identify the active route or section; Administration and Check-in do not. On Check-in, Scan and Lookup look equally selected even when one is already open.  
   **Why it matters:** Staff must rely on the page heading instead of the navigation to confirm where they are.  
   **Fix:** Mark the current Admin and Check-in links with `aria-current="page"` and a visual treatment that matches the event workflow menu.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

2. **[P2] Several Admin and Check-in links have small tap areas.** At 390px, their link boxes are about 24px high, compared with the 44px targets used in public and event navigation.  
   **Why it matters:** Small targets are harder to tap reliably on a phone during trial-day use.  
   **Fix:** Give these links at least a 44px hit area while keeping the styling compact.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

3. **[P2] The open mobile Event tools menu occupies most of the screen.** At 390px, its nine links form a list about 594px tall, pushing the page task well below the initial view.  
   **Why it matters:** Staff have to scan many destinations before returning to the page they were working on.  
   **Fix:** Keep the three group labels, but disclose links by group on narrow screens and open the current group by default.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

4. **[P2] Registration section links scroll away during the long form.** The four links appear above the form, but aren’t reachable without scrolling back up.  
   **Why it matters:** Parents must scroll past earlier sections to jump to later details or eligibility.  
   **Fix:** Keep a compact section navigator reachable while scrolling, without covering focused fields or changing the single-page flow.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

5. **[P3] Public staff access is visually centered in the desktop header.** At 1280px, the Staff & volunteers group sits near the middle, drawing attention away from public navigation.  
   **Why it matters:** These routes are useful, but secondary to the public trial task.  
   **Fix:** Keep both links and their group label visible, but align the group to the right as a quieter utility area.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Volunteer using a phone:** Check-in has Scan and Lookup links but no selected state, and their tap areas are short. The volunteer must infer the current mode from the page heading.
- **Committee member on Event operations:** Opening Event tools displays all nine links in a tall list, pushing the event controls down.
- **Parent navigating registration:** The section map helps at the start, but disappears as they move through the long form.
