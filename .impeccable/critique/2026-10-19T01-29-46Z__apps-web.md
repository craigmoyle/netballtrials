## Design Specificity Verdict

The interface is specific to Chisholm netball trials: its ranked positions, court/team colors, check-in tasks, and planning workflows reflect the actual work. The blue/plum identity and restrained “Sideline Clipboard” approach are consistent with `DESIGN.md`.

The visual language is more conventional than distinctive—system typography, forms, tables, and dividers—but that restraint suits an operational product. The best opportunity is improving task hierarchy and recovery, rather than adding decoration.

## Overall Impression

The core flows are coherent and safer than a generic event-management tool. The remaining weaknesses show up at the edges: parents discover required fields late, volunteers have a scanner recovery gap, and staff can lose unsaved schedule edits. The interface is visually calm, but some important tasks are still longer and less recoverable than they need to be.

## What’s Working

1. **Registration explains the decision before requesting details.** Families see the fee, trial logistics, privacy information, age range, and eligibility criteria.
2. **Consequential Admin actions are explained near their controls.** Event closure and check-in/PIN changes state their effects and preserve operation-specific feedback.
3. **The operational layout adapts well.** At 390px the workflow navigation collapses cleanly, court colors stack, and game tables remain within the page instead of forcing horizontal overflow.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Required registration fields aren’t clear before submission.**  
   `RegisterPage.tsx:323-417` uses `noValidate`; only the first position is marked required, while other server-required fields have no required cue or required semantics. A parent can reach the end before discovering omissions.  
   **Fix:** Mark every server-required field visibly and programmatically, then validate the full required set while preserving the focused error summary and field-level messages.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

2. **[P1] Undoing a scanned check-in can prevent scanning that same ticket again.**  
   `ScanPage.tsx:90-91` suppresses repeated tokens, but undo at `ScanPage.tsx:121-131` clears the result without resetting `lastToken`. The success message says the player may be checked in again, yet rescanning the same QR is ignored until another token is seen.  
   **Fix:** Rearm the scanner for that token after a successful undo; add a regression test for scan → undo → scan the same QR.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

3. **[P2] Manual lookup gives no feedback while searching or when no player matches.**  
   `LookupPanel.tsx:19-30, 78-90` debounces a search but renders neither a pending state nor a no-results message. A slow or empty response looks like a broken blank list.  
   **Fix:** Announce that lookup is in progress and show a clear empty-result state once the request completes.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

4. **[P2] Full Game List edits can be lost through event navigation.**  
   The page identifies edits as local until saved, but the event-workflow links remain usable while a round or suffix is dirty (`FullGameListPage.tsx:763-764`; `EventWorkflowNav.tsx:58-77`). Navigating away can discard a time-consuming schedule change without confirmation.  
   **Fix:** Warn before leaving while unsaved edits exist, with clear stay/leave choices.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

5. **[P2] The public trial listing gives little eligibility context before “Register.”**  
   The listing shows division, date, venue, arrival, whistle, and fee, but not the event’s age bounds or a direct eligibility cue (`PublicHomePage.tsx:35-45`). Families may start registration before discovering whether their player qualifies.  
   **Fix:** Add a compact event-derived age cue or an eligibility link on each listing, without duplicating the full policy.  
   **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan, first-time parent:** The listing doesn’t show age eligibility, and most required fields are not identified until after submission.
- **Sam, accessibility-dependent user:** Registration has good labels and error focus, but manual lookup has no announced loading or no-results state; the scanner’s repeated-token behavior conflicts with its undo message.
- **Casey, interrupted mobile user:** Registration is a long phone flow. The app warns before leaving, but the form cannot be resumed after a refresh. That is a deliberate memory-only constraint, so the warning should remain prominent rather than introducing browser persistence.

## Minor Observations

- The eligibility criteria are packed into a long sentence; the browser overlay flagged roughly 90-character rendered lines at desktop width. Shorter sentences or a compact list would scan more easily.
- The equal-spacing `heading-rhythm` findings may be heuristic noise; the section headings remain identifiable.
- The selection-policy link still falls back to `#` when an event has no policy URL, although it looks actionable.
- The public header exposes Admin and volunteer check-in links alongside the family flow; that may be intentional for shared-device access.

## Questions to Consider

- Should event eligibility be surfaced on the public listing, or kept on the registration page?
- For Full Game List, should the default show one round, or all rounds with an explicit filter?
